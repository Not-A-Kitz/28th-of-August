28th of August

Part 3 : Transition to a more recent timing

Now, you probably think that I am only talking about the past, about a time that did not last long, and you would be right. You know, I often wondered if the you I was in love with wasn’t only the you I got to know back then, the one who stopped existing over time.

For months, I kept wondering why I was still there if the person I was in love with was no more.The truth is that I learnt to know a new person. One with a bad temper, not as present, one who ignores messages… But one who is also tired. One with a big heart still and who deserves love and attention. And I also learnt to love them.

~ Bring the sun in ~

This song. You have no idea just how many times I listened to it while thinking about you. At this point, I wouldn’t even be surprised if it makes it to my top 5 at the end of the year. What we had was brief, but I cherish it more than anything.

But you know…

I still love all the little things you do, that’s what makes this special.

You know, I was a little sad when you sent me that message saying that I scared you with the name change in discord, because yours had remained the same, even though that name wasn't really anything actual anymore.

But then, I saw that I was still on your pinned conversations. Then, I saw that my name on your Telegram was still the same, and was still pinned as well.

Given we’re not talking so often, you had countless moments to change that ; but you didn’t. That single thing alone proved to me once again that it was something else than just you not wanting to chat anymore.

You’re going through a lot of changes in your everyday life, and at the same time you have to deal with all the daily attacks. You’re taking care of the house and your conflictual relationship with your parents… So I am glad that you’re taking the time still to reconnect with your friends and think of yourself a little, for once.

I am aware that I’m not a top priority in your life right now, but at the same time I’m glad to see that you did not completely forget about me. I don’t know if you still see me as a best friend, or what’s the nature of your feelings for me. But I am glad to still be in your life, because I want you in mine.

So when you do those tiny little things, when you show your emotions, feelings, thoughts and bits of moments of life with me, it makes me happy.

When I’m the first you show your work at the office, when you tell me about your meeting with your friends, what’s going on at work, or even just when you tell me about video games and your plans for a certain period of time.

It makes me happy. It makes me happy because you are still including me in your life. Even if we’re nowhere talking as much as we used to when we met, even if there is a lot of info you’re still only keeping for yourself, I am glad to still be in your life. To be a part of it, somehow.
