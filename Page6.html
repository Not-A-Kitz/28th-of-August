Part 2 : Getting to know each other better

Do you remember that time, on a Yale’s movie night where you suddenly called me? You said you wanted to watch the movie on call, as if we were together in real life. I’ll be honest, it was hard to follow. First, because it’s hard to concentrate on two things at once, and second because I was far too excited. Still, it was super romantic. You proved to me we don’t have to be in the same room to spend some time together and do some things for each other.

And even before that, do you remember our first Yale’s movie night? You would come back late from work so we would sometimes end up watching movies just the two of us.

Now, to be frank, I hate repetitions (not in the way that you do, but still). The idea of watching the same movie twice in a row feels like a nightmare. Though, watching it again with you felt like a blessing. It was funnier, it was also a way to catch more details but more importantly, it was being with you that mattered the most.

There was this time where we watched Sherlock Junior. Pretty funny movie honestly, and also quite relatable. It’s one of my favorites from those on the list. Still, one moment stood up for me.

It was Sherlock in the cinema, watching a movie where the character ends up with the girl he likes, and tries to do the same. Yet, he’s only copying the moves until he just cannot anymore. He’s some kind of loser and is in reality, not very good at flirting.

Seeing him, you immediately told me he reminds you of yourself. Coming from someone showing so much confidence, it was surprising, but also amusing because that night, I confessed not being any better.

In the end, we really were like two Sherlock Junior ; two losers having a thing for each other but unable to do something about it aha.

Speaking of movies, it also reminds me of when you used to stream those you really like. It’s something that I miss, to be honest.

Sure, it’s something that I can do on my own, but it has another flavour when you’re the one doing it. You always have facts about movies, characters, actors…

You get excited for a scene or another and discovering the film through your eyes is deeply exciting. It makes me feel closer to you. In a way, it’s as if you were finally allowing me into your intimacy.

...Does this jukebox ring a bell? Maybe it does. Maybe not. Well, it was used on the fic I wrote over a year ago.

It was something silly, important in my eyes but also light. I wasn't expecting it to turn into some kind of manifestation.

Yet, you came into my life. I wasn't expecting it from you at first, but you turned out to be that charming and caring non-binary. Sweet, clever, handsome and desirable.

I remember how you took the time to read it as soon as I told you about it. You made a full analysis and indeed, found some ressemblances between you and the Rio I depicted. That was adorable.

There was this night where you told me you wanted to watch Sweeney Todd. It was a movie you really like (and I understand why, given the protagonist) but also, it was stressing me a little since I grew up being terrified by this story.

Still, you stayed with me every single second of the movie. As much as you appreciate this film, you had this whole discussion with me. I told you about my deepest scares and traumas and you were very sweet about it, protective but also attentive.

You went above and beyond during a moment that was not exactly the good one, but didn’t complain about it. You made it safe and reassuring. That night, I remember thinking « They are a safe space. I could just fall asleep in their arms, make love with them and make it a truly enjoyable moment for the very first time. »

There was this time, where you were streaming something. You’re always very careful and use different web browsers to not mix everything up or expose yourself. Yet, that day I noticed a search of yours. You had researched an expression you just used to me a few hours prior, to make sure it was the most adapted to the situation.

It’s the kind of small attention I wasn’t expecting from you. You did not just tell me nice things, you took the time to research them to make it the most accurate. That was probably one of the most adorable things I’ve ever seen. Realizing that I was this important to you made my heart jump in happiness.

Speaking of deep conversations, do you remember that night where we spoke almost only in French? It was in a group with Jessi on it. Poor girl aha, she has to deal with our mess. Though, I gotta say it was kinda reassuring knowing someone else was with us, because I could’ve just died on the spot being left alone with such an impressive and charismatic being.

I feel like, for maybe the very first time, you opened your heart to me. You showed me just how ready you were to make some efforts to make things work out. For something between us to exist, to be created, and to be good. You took every precaution to not jeopardize a potential future between us.

You took in consideration my feelings, yours, our current obstacles and even projected yourself into the future, by wanting to go deep into French for me, even though it wasn’t a language that was more interesting than that to you. Mon p’tit coeur.

I feel like such a reaction is anybody’s dream, and you served it to me. If I could kiss you, I would have. To be honest, it was also that night that made me think, “I need to learn Ukrainian. I need to study that beautiful language and be worthy of them.” Моє маленьке кохання.

There was this time where we were together watching Tár, I don’t know if you remember. Now, I hate long movies and I hate classical music. Yet, I sat there with you the whole time ; precisely because it was with you.

At a certain moment, Cate Blanchett spoke in German, and while I’m not sure what she said exactly, you wrote in the chat "ich kriege dich”. It was at the beginning of August. I had a big fat crush on you already, and reading this in chat made me freeze.

I immediately translated it and was left speechless. Was it coming from the movie? Was it for me? Was it just random? I couldn’t tell, but this very random event did something to me.

And maybe I was a fool for overanalyzing everything like that, perhaps it was meaningless, but you know what? I don’t care. You’re important and every single thing you say matters to me. You could just be drunk and send me a succession of random words not even knowing what you’re typing that I would search for any explanation, even if I knew it was just you being too drunk to type.

You have that mind I can never decipher, as hard as I’m trying and as annoying as it is sometimes, it is also extremely attractive. That one night when I said that I wanted your brain, your passwords and well.. Your heart. You told me you didn’t realize right away, and that was funny.

Well, I do still mean what I said that night. Or should I say, partially? Yeah, because I don’t care about your passwords. All I want is you and has always been you.

.. Okay, back to German. Turns out, I was right because the next time you spoke German on the server, it was for a very different thing.

You know I’m a total noob in this language, but I do know some basic words or sentences. So when we were in the middle of watching Region, flirting for fun but also not really (a thing that only us and Jessi knew about)... When you told me these three little words in German, everything stopped.

You made my heart beat so fast it exploded on the spot. I hid under my sheets, the phone close to my nose reading over and over again what you wrote. The video was still playing as I was the one streaming but you can be sure I haven’t followed a single thing of what was going on.

I wasn’t sure if that was a silly trick, a joke or a real fact, but reading this word from the person of my dream felt like a fever dream. Today, I feel bad for not saying a thing and letting the others believe even for a second it may be one-sided.

Maybe you would’ve said it was simply a joke, nothing serious or that I didn’t understand properly, but I was ready to take the risk of being humiliated forever if this means showing the world how happy I was for sharing feelings with you.

Not long after your face revealed to Jessi and me, you started livestreaming on face camera. I know it was not only just for the sake of being there, but also to provoke a reaction in me, and I can assure you the job was really well executed.

Seeing you on my screen was like seeing your favorite dessert, the most good looking you have ever seen, being right in front of you, being called to be eaten ̶o̶u̶t̶ while not being able to touch it. I guess you can call that a torture.

I remember that time after Jessi’s birthday when you texted me. You said you had your eyes stuck on my neck for a long time, and god, it was already hot and I was drunk, but you made it worse. My room turned into hell; because of the heat outside and in me, but also because of that temptation you were creating.

I think that night was mostly you and me. The others were around, chatting, sometimes coming on camera / call… But I couldn’t care less about them. Maybe it is selfish, maybe it is bad but on Jessi’s birthday, the only person I cared about was you.

Looking back to it, I cannot say I regret this decision. I don’t think they deserved anything more, any of them, while you’re still in my life and worth all of my attention.

To this day, even when we’re not talking, you’re still on my mind every single day.
