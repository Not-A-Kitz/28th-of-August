Part 4 : What's coming next

You do those small little things that do not seem to mean much at first glance but mean everything to me. For example, how you can not talk but still send tiktoks. How, while scrolling, your brain still says “ah, I should send it to Flora”.

Now, that’s my shame for discovering them late, as I avoided tiktok for a while to avoid the pain the AgathaRio edits on my for you page would cause me.. But coming back with dozens of shares from you filled me with joy. Truly.

I like when you’re streaming music because whatever you’re playing, you always end up sneaking one or two Ukrainian songs. Now, maybe that’s just a coincidence or you’re simply hitting shuffles. Still, because of you I can feed my playlist.

Given how little you listen to those, I refuse to believe that’s a coincidence, and that’s one of the little things I adore about you.

3 elements to find.

The singer says that, no matter all the problems, no matter all the pain, he will never stop loving that person even though she’s falling. He says that he will always be there for her, to take her hand and lift her up. For her, he would do the first, the second and the third step (troisième pas). That is just how I feel about you. I won’t let you down, I don’t want to.

Yeah, sometimes you’re colder than snow, you’re distant, hurting even, and you have a bad temper. But you’re doing your best, all the time. For you, for me, for everyone. You’re just a human trying to live, well, survive even.

No one can be blamed for that. And certainly no one should ever let you down for that.

When January came and we weren’t as close as we used to already, you surprised me by taking part in this surprise birthday party. You drew for me this amazing drawing of my favorite genshin character.

Not only that, you also confessed you had asked your best friend to give you advice so you could make this the best drawing possible. Knowing not only you spent literal DAYS on it, but also that you asked people for it to be perfect makes it even more special.

And I’m so damn grateful.

A month later, you also did something that stunned me. I didn’t really pay attention to what day it was because of how early in the morning it was but you sent me a screen of a notification you received.

Usually, you just send me the said notification or message cropped or in the full app. This time, you sent me the full picture from your lock screen. I saw that not only you kept my timezone on your phone, but also that my name on your Telegram had remained the same.

It sent my heart a shockwave; but again I did not realize which day we were.

At the same moment, the Lantern Rite was happening in Genshin. In the morning, I sent you diverse messages from the restricted system to tell you that I still care, a lot, about you ; because that day, it was Valentine’s day.

We didn’t talk a lot that day, but at 23, even though it was already the next day for you, I decided that I should still say something. So, I told you that, despite everything, I still loved you.

That message scared me to send, not just because of the fear of being rejected, but also because months prior, you told me that my loving words were making you uncomfortable. My biggest fear was making you feel this awful feeling once again.

Instead, you simply thanked me.

I’m still not quite sure if it was a “you’re too late whatchu doing?”, a “Um okay thanks but let’s keep some distance” or more of a “thank you for reminding me”, but I personally choose to believe it was the last one.

That same day, well, on Valentine’s day, I had already left the server. Still, you wanted us to spend some time together.

And so, you asked your best friend to lend me her identity so I could use it and be with you. You had no reason to do that, as it was my own fault for leaving. Still, you created strategies so we could be together, and that’s the most adorable thing ever.

I don’t know if you realize just how much you’re doing for me, constantly. Even when times are hard for you, even when your mental health is at its lowest and you’re losing hope in everything, you’re still doing your best for me.

I just wish there was a way for me to be there for you just the same. I just wish you would let me stay with you. I just wish you could tell me about your days, what was good, what was not, that you could let me support you, reassure you when needed, simply be there for you.

When I lost Farouche, it was really difficult for me. I tried my best to appear strong for the sake of everyone, but it was destroying me from the inside. I kept most things for myself and tried to move on. Still, when it happened, I really needed your voice, your presence, your reassurance.

I called you, knowing you were very probably busy, as you were working, but I still wanted to try anyway. To my surprise, you picked up the phone. You stayed with me talking and trying to cheer me up. You had clients, and I just know damn well that if it had been anyone else, in any other context, you would’ve just said something like “Don’t you see I’m busy??”.

But you stayed with me. You alternated between taking care of me and customers. You gave up free time on your phone just so we could be together. I know that’s a lot coming from you, and for that I will forever be grateful.

The next few days, you sent me whatever to just make me think of something else. It worked, and that’s something I really needed. Then, you started streaming and even spent a whole entire day streaming games just for me.

You could have done literally anything but you stayed with me, and I cherish that more than anything in this world. Thank you for that, really.

You kept doing this for about a week and it did help, way more than you could think.

What I didn’t know though, was that you were also going through something tough at the same time. You learnt that you would have to leave the shop and find a new job. So you did, you worked two jobs at once no matter how exhausting it was.

Not knowing what was going on was painful, as I couldn’t tell if something happened to you, if you were fine, mad at me, just busy or depressed.

In the end, you told me what was happening after three weeks. Three weeks you endured alone, right after you took all that time to take care of me.

It must have been exhausting, and I really wish you would’ve let me take care of you in return.

After I parted ways with the others and the Yale’s Movie Server in general, you took the time to build a new one. You could have just say “fuck off” and leave all of this to avoid more problems. But you didn’t. You created a new server from scratch so we could be together.

Now, I know this wasn’t made just for me, thank god actually. But still, seeing how you didn’t want to give up on this and just retreat on Mara’s server, that you were really willing to invest yourself on this made me happy.

Seeing how you were ready to build something new, bring in some people, create a whole new community in which you wanted me to be an administrator showed me your trust and affection. Thank you for that.

Last but not least, there was this time where you sent me this gif. You told me that you liked to lick your partner’s neck to turn them on.

Obviously, it made me jealous and I took it badly. But at the same time, I gotta admit that even just this slight idea kinda turned me on. The simple fact of imagining you doing this to me made me boil from the inside.

My reaction wasn’t the best, but I refuse to believe this came from nowhere. You are clever, you knew exactly what you were doing. Seeing how months after anything happened between us, you were still attempting (and succeeding) to make me feel things for you was both an honor and a relief.

Thank you.
