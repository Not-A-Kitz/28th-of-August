Part 5 : The conclusion

I had this dream the night of the 28th of may. That’s funny, it was exactly 3 months ago. That’s how it went ;

We were in Kyiv. You came close to me, so close I could feel you breathing. Facing you, I couldn’t reciprocate — you left me breathless. My eyes were locked on your lips. I was trembling. A few centimeters were separating us. I was dying to feel your lips on mine. Experiencing the taste of your saliva. But I couldn’t be sure this was your intention. Perhaps you only wanted to whisper to me a few words. Still, this moment filled me up with joy. So much in fact, that I could’ve faint. Because as much as I would've died for one of your kiss, being close to you was enough. Even if I can only experience your kisses while daydreaming, knowing you’re right around the corner makes me happy. Knowing you’re here, somewhere on this earth, alive makes me euphoric.

You pretend you don’t give a fuck about anything or anyone, but we all know that’s not the truth. You keep all your feelings, your emotions to yourself. You do everything to be strong and it makes me want to suffocate you with a big hug. To protect you.

Let me kiss your forehead and tell you that everything will be alright.

It’s extremely rare to see you surrender and let me enter your intimity, even a little bit. Like people say, you need to give time some time. I don’t feel like letting you go. Even when we’re not talking, when I leave you some space, you’re still always on the side of my head, and I doubt that will ever change.

I want to be your friend, or anything you would like to be.

Everything that I quoted today was about us two, but I also need you to know that even on your own, you are extremely adorable. For example, when you stop midway to take pictures of flowers, or the birds you come across. When the sky is pretty so you take your phone to immortalize it... Who could’ve thought such a sweet soul was hiding under a big personality like yours?

One day, you told me that whenever you were showing your drawings, you felt like a child. A child who is proud to show what he did, but also was very vulnerable.

That is the most realistic and touching thing you ever told me I believe.

I know that you want to be strong, and trust me, you are. But I also know that deep inside of you resides that kid who wants to be loved for who they are. That kid is mad at the entire world, and I think they’re actually right for that. That child, I’m dreaming of taking him into a big warm embrace, to be his shield. This child in you, I want to cherish him, and show him just how loved he is. Even if he rejects me, even if he refuses.

This is not me saying I’ll go over your consent, but rather that I’ll always be there, no matter you want it or not, and will always be there if you ever need me.

I know. I made a lot of mistakes and did not always behave right with you. For that, you have all my most sincere apologies.

I wanted to do my best, and sometimes did the exact opposite and hurt you. Though, please, you need to know one thing ; not one second I stopped loving you.

I still hope that one day, I’ll wake up and hear someone grumble in the house in the morning. I want that someone to be you. Then, you’ll get sweeter once more awoken, and I’ll kiss you before we both leave for work.

Such a silly dream, right? But at least, dreams are free.

All of this just to say; you’re the kind of person who needs some time; I know that. I also know that for you, this year has been quite a mess and the least of your priorities right now would be a love story. Still, if one day you feel ready for that, we can always go back to where we left things.

Maybe you think that we hurt ourselves too much for that, or that you don’t want me to wait eternally for you. Both are valuable reasons. Though, I want you to know that I’m fully aware of everything, and despite that, I’m still willing to give us a chance if that’s what you want.

I tried to stop loving you, but so far it doesn’t look like that’s possible. By making this website, my ideas were simple ; exposing all of my feelings to you, so you can choose to either accept or refuse them.

You don’t have to give me an answer now, or any answer at all to be honest. I will stop talking about those feelings I have for you, about everything from now on.

This project was important for me because I cannot move on without you knowing everything. Now, if you tell me you only want us to remain friends, I would be able to accept it fully.

Whatever your answer would be, I won’t be mad. I’ll accept it, and thank you for making it to this very last page.

Thank you also for everything you did during that complicated year.

There is one thing that you need to know. That promise I made you a year ago is worth a lifetime. And if when the time comes, you’re not up for a date or not even up to see me, it’s alright. I would still take the time to enjoy this beautiful country and improve my knowledge of the language.

I can wait for you as long as you’d need it. Though, if you already know that your answer is a no, I’ll leave you alone. I’ll forget about my love and move on. Still, you are important to me as a friend, and if you still want me around, I’ll be happy to just be your buddy.

A point was reached where it’s hard to follow you and know what you want, to be honest. I take my distances to not suffocate you, and at the same time I’m scared you’d think I don’t care when it’s not the case. So now, the cards are in your hand.

Thank you for this year.

A year ago, I was updating your discord note.

Rome wasn't made in a day, but this website was made in three months.

Happy 28th of august.
